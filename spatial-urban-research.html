Spatial Urban Research.

The Modern Mamak

The Modern Mamak explores the evolving role of Singapore's traditional provision stores as community spaces, examining how they can adapt to changing social and economic conditions while preserving their cultural and neighbourhood significance.

Viasta | Viaduct Green Festival

Viasta is a community-driven design initiative that transforms an underutilised viaduct into a vibrant public space, fostering social connections, resident participation, and neighbourhood identity through collaborative events and activities.

Regenerative Cities Studies

Regenerative City is an ongoing research exploring how cities can move beyond sustainability by identifying the principles, strategies, and spatial characteristics that support regenerative urban development.
