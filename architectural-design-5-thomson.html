5 Thomson Ridge |Ballet Studio

5 Thomson Ballet Studio is a commercial renovation project that transformed the upper floors of a heritage shophouse into a contemporary ballet studio. Delivered over the course of a year, the project encompassed architectural and interior design, statutory approvals, tender coordination, and construction management, culminating in the successful completion and handover of the studio. As part of the project team, I played a key role in guiding the project from design development through construction. My involvement included managing statutory submissions to authorities such as BCA, LTA, URA, NEA, PUB, and NParks, preparing tender and construction documentation, and coordinating closely with clients, consultants, contractors, and fabricators throughout the process. I also contributed to the development of the interior design through spatial planning, 3D visualisations, and detailed carpentry drawings, ensuring that design intent was carried through to execution. Through regular site inspections and stakeholder coordination, I helped oversee the delivery of the project while balancing regulatory requirements, construction quality, budget considerations, and the client's vision.

Project Details.

1. Design Concept and Inspiration.

As an all-girls ballet studio, the client envisioned a light and elegant atmosphere defined by soft pink tones, seamless curves, and naturally flowing spaces. Maximizing daylight was a key design goal, achieved through full-height windows that open the interior to natural light. The program also includes restrooms and showers on both the second and attic floors, along with a custom-designed reception area that complements the studio's gentle and cohesive aesthetic.

2. Plan and Construction Progress.

Throughout the planning and construction process, I coordinated the design development, statutory submissions, tender documentation, and site execution, working closely with consultants, contractors, and the client to ensure the project was delivered smoothly and in line with the design intent.
