Visual Communications and Exhibition Works.

Exploring graphic design, branding, exhibitions, and visual storytelling across research, public engagement, and the built environment.

1. Viasta 4.0

As part of the organizing and design team, I designed and developed the publicity materials for Viasta 4.0, creating a refreshed visual identity aimed at engaging and attracting a younger audience. Moving away from the original marketing style, the graphics drew inspiration from familiar digital interfaces such as alarm apps and CAPTCHA systems to create a playful, relatable aesthetic. While introducing this contemporary visual language, the design retained the established green palette to align with Viasta’s sustainability-focused identity.

2. World Cities Summit (2026)
